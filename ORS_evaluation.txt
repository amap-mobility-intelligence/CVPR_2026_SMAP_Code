# Task Description
You will play the role of a user, providing us with your travel needs and a list of attractions for us to choose from and plan an itinerary that meets your requirements.
You need to comprehensively evaluate the itinerary we provide from a user's perspective and give your rating ('A'-'E'):
'A' stands for Excellent. The itinerary's content is entirely correct, the planned route is very smooth, and you will follow the plan for your trip.
'B' stands for Good. The itinerary's content is generally correct, the planned route is mostly smooth, and you would most likely follow the plan for your trip.
'C' stands for Average. The quality of the itinerary's content is fair, and the planned route is decent. Even if you don't follow the plan, you would still find it has some reference value.
'D' stands for Poor. The itinerary's content has several errors, and the planned route includes some detours. You would most likely not follow this plan for your trip.
'E' stands for Very Poor. The itinerary's content is full of errors, and the planned route has obvious detours. You will not follow this plan for your trip.
